How it works

  1. Seven prompts a day. Each clock starts after a three-second descent and runs 25 seconds.
  2. Type one player who fits. A wrong name says "not on the board" and the clock keeps running, so try again.
  3. Every right answer has points fixed before the day goes out. The rarer the answer, the more it's worth.
  4. Each point sinks you 10 meters. A perfect dive reaches 7,000 m.
