The Kawhi Anomaly

The biggest rebound ever recorded by a 34-year-old. Late surges usually fade. Which half of his will last?

Kawhi Leonard went from a good starter to the fifth-best player in the league per minute, at 34. No player 34 or older has ever jumped that far in one season.

Eight players have surged to star level after 31. None kept three-quarters of the gain the next year. His bigger role usually lasts. His hot shooting usually doesn't.
