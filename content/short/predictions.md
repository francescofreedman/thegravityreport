Every call we make below gets a probability with it, and every probability goes into the Forecast Ledger, where it will get scored after the season. When a sportsbook has a price, we quote it with the book and the date, and we say how far away we are from it.

All of our team calls now come from our new model, TORQUE, which we introduced on October 14.[1](https://thegravityreport.com/the30/) TORQUE projects a win total for every team, and it misses by an average of about 7 wins. Vegas usually beats a model, so on our team calls we didn't just take TORQUE's number. We weight each prediction 40% on what TORQUE says and 60% on what Vegas says. That means when TORQUE and Vegas agree on a team, there's nothing to call: our number would just land on the betting line. So we only made calls where the two disagree, because that's where our model thinks it sees something the market doesn't. A dagger (†) marks a call whose roster situation is not settled.

## The title

!call 34% | Oklahoma City wins the 2026–27 championship. | TORQUE puts the Thunder first with a score of 59.4, 5.8 points clear of the next team, which projects them at 67 wins for the year. No other team is projected above 52 wins. That's a massive gap.[1](https://thegravityreport.com/the30/) DraftKings prices them even with San Antonio at +270, an implied 27% chance.[2](https://www.si.com/betting/2026-27-nba-championship-odds-for-every-team-spurs-thunder-co-favorites-knicks-tied-with-philly) Our simulation of the season gives them a 44% chance of winning it all, so by combining our two numbers, we put our final prediction at 34%.

!call 6% | The Finals is Oklahoma City against Detroit. | This is the most likely Finals matchup predicted by TORQUE. The Pistons are the highest-rated team in the East at 53.3, which comes out to 51 projected wins.[1](https://thegravityreport.com/the30/) The betting market doesn't see it that way. BetMGM has Detroit at +900 to win the East (tied for fourth in the conference) and the Thunder at +145 in the West, which multiply to about a 4% chance.[3](https://sports.betmgm.com/en/blog/nba/nba-eastern-conference-odds-bm23/),[4](https://sports.betmgm.com/en/blog/nba/nba-western-conference-odds-bm23/) Our simulation gives it a 10% chance, which brings our final prediction to 6%.

## How these are graded

Award calls resolve on the NBA's announcements. The title and Finals calls resolve on the 2027 Finals. Win totals, the games call and the triple-double call resolve on Basketball Reference's final regular-season tables. The trade call resolves on the NBA transaction log at the deadline.
