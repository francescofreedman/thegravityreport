## Recurring series

Each series has an off switch in `social_settings` (set it to `0`); the Worker checks it every run.
Series that come from schedule.json are also removed by editing `scripts/social/make_schedule.py` and rerunning it.

| Series | When | Comes from | Turn it off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Player of the day | Daily, 10 AM ET (picked each morning from last night's box scores) | Worker, automatic (social/potd.js) | `potd_on = 0` in social_settings (admin page toggle) |
| The 644 on your team | Daily, 6 PM ET, one team a day for 30 days from Sat Oct 24, 2026 (TORQUE order) | schedule.json (kits `<date>-team-<code>`, make_kit.py team all) | `series_team644 = 0` in social_settings, or TEAM644_START = None in make_schedule.py |
| Is the model winning? | Mondays, 9 AM ET, from Mon Oct 26, 2026 (each week's kit is written Sunday night / Monday morning) | schedule.json (kit `<monday>-ledger-tracker`, written weekly by `python3 scripts/social/ledger_tracker.py --schedule`, then commit + push; Basketball Reference can't be fetched from the Worker) | `series_tracker = 0` in social_settings, or TRACKER_ON = False in make_schedule.py |
| Beating the projection | Wednesdays, 1 PM ET (skipped, and marked, while teams average under 5 games) | Worker, automatic (social/series.js: ESPN standings vs TORQUE win projections); X, Bluesky, Threads only | `series_pace = 0` in social_settings |
| Best games of the week | Fridays, 1 PM ET (last Friday through Thursday) | Worker, automatic (social/series.js: top 5 Game Scores among players in The 644 from ESPN box scores, cached per day; X, Bluesky, Threads only) | `series_best = 0` in social_settings |
| Who's higher? | Saturdays, 11 AM ET; the answer Sundays, 11 AM ET | Worker, automatic (social/series.js: two adjacent ranks in the top 100 of The 644, rotating by week; an X poll, 'reply with your pick' elsewhere; the answer post names both ranks and scores) | `series_poll = 0` in social_settings |
| From the archive | Sundays, 12 PM ET, one Deep Dive a week from Sun Nov 1, 2026 (Deep Dive order) | schedule.json (kits `<sunday>-article-<slug>`, make_kit.py article all --date 2026-11-01: tracking frame, angle 2) | `series_archive = 0` in social_settings, or ARCHIVE_START = None in make_schedule.py |
| Instagram + Threads hand-off (not a post) | 15–30 minutes before any Instagram or Threads row is due | Worker, automatic (social/handoff.js): emails the caption, the images and a 3-line how-to to HANDOFF_TO; the row shows 'Sent to your phone'. The Worker never posts to Instagram or Threads itself. | remove the platform from SOCIAL_MANUAL_PLATFORMS in wrangler.jsonc (then its posts need that platform's API keys) |
| Metrics panel (not a post) | Weekly, first cron tick of each ISO week | Worker, automatic (social/metrics.js: Bluesky followers from the public API, Threads/Instagram only with tokens, X left blank; posts sent that week from social_log; shown at the top of /admin/schedule/ when unlocked) | `series_metrics = 0` in social_settings |
