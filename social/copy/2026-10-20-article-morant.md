Ja Morant went from the 97th percentile of the league in 2021–22 to playing 79 of a possible 246 games in the three seasons since 2023. We measured every season of his career with one rating system, applied the same way to every player, and the fall is mostly an availability story. The 644 ranks him #140, and that number prices the risk.

The collapse was about missing, not choosing: measured across shot zones, 88% of his 2025–26 efficiency crash came from worse shot-making and only 12% from where he shot. His three-point slump covers just 20-of-85 attempts, too few to tell decline from ordinary variance. The one split that moved beyond doubt went up: his free-throw shooting improved to .897.

Our verdict: #140 is a defensible price for the delivery risk, and it is probably the wrong forecast of the talent.
