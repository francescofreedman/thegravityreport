We asked ourselves whether #140 is the right price for Ja Morant, so we tested our own number seven ways: overrides off, equal season weights, different anchors, the reliability stack deleted. Every variation landed him between #112 and #147, meaning that #140 isn't an artifact of any single judgment call.

The absence ledger explains #140 better than any shooting chart: 79 of a possible 246 games across three seasons, which is 32.1%. The 2023–24 season alone had a 25-game suspension, a nine-game return, and then 48 games lost to the shoulder.

Our verdict: #140 is a defensible price for the delivery risk, and it is probably the wrong forecast of the talent. Both statements are the same mathematics, read in two directions.
