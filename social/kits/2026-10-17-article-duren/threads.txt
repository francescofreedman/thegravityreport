Every other player in our top ten took at least 22% of his shots from three. Jalen Duren took none, and still graded 7th in the league per minute. The first top-ten non-shooter in ten years.

In 2004–05 four of the top ten never shot threes, Kevin Garnett first among them. From 2015–16 through 2024–25 the count was zero.
