Kawhi Leonard, at 34 years old, went from a good starter to the fifth-best player in the league per minute. His impact score, how much he helps per minute versus an average NBA minute, went from +0.86 to +2.17. No player 34 or older has ever gained that much in one season.
---
Historically, only eight players have surged to star level after 31, and none of them kept three-quarters of that gain the next season; Steve Nash kept the most, 45%. Kawhi's surge came in two parts, a heavier role and hotter shooting, and those two parts age differently.
---
Before tip-off, we put four calls on his season, and each one gets graded in public:
65% his true shooting falls below .610 (he shot .629 last season)
60% his usage stays at 30% or more
50% he stays top 8 in The 644, July 2027 edition
30% he finishes top 8 in Box Plus/Minus
---
