Ja Morant went from the 97th percentile in 2021–22 to playing 79 of a possible 246 games in the three seasons since 2023. We measured every season of his career with one rating system, and the fall is mostly an availability story. The 644 ranks him #140 to price that risk.
---
The collapse was about missing, not choosing: measured across shot zones, 88% of his 2025–26 efficiency crash came from worse shot-making and only 12% from where he shot. His three-point slump covers just 20-of-85 attempts, too few to tell decline from ordinary variance.
---
The one split that moved beyond doubt went up: his free-throw shooting improved to .897. We published this on July 10. The full piece is free:
