Ja Morant went from the 97th percentile of the league in 2021–22 to playing 79 of a possible 246 games in the three seasons since 2023. The fall is mostly an availability story, and The 644 ranks him #140 to price that risk.

The collapse was about missing, not choosing: measured across shot zones, 88% of his efficiency crash came from worse shot-making and only 12% from where he shot.
