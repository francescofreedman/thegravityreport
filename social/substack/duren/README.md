# Paste "The Last of His Kind" into Substack (about 15 minutes)

1. Open `post.html` in your browser (double-click it).
2. In Substack: **New post**. Title: `The Last of His Kind`. Subtitle: `For ten seasons no player who refused to shoot threes was in the top ten, until Jalen Duren.`
3. Back in the browser tab: select all (Cmd+A), copy (Cmd+C), paste into the Substack body (Cmd+V).
4. Where the forecasts are, drag in `bars.png`. Alt text: `Registered forecasts: He stays top 15 in The 644, 80%; He posts the best BPM among traditional centers, 60%.`
5. Check the two figures loaded (they come from thegravityreport.com). Settings → set the post's canonical URL to `https://thegravityreport.com/notes/duren/` if Substack offers it.
6. Preview the email, then publish. Nothing here publishes on its own.
