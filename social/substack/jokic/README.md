1. Open `post.html` in your browser (double-click it).
2. In Substack: **New post**. Title: `Chasing Seven`. Subtitle: `By our model, the best player in the league for six straight years. One more would tie Michael Jordan's record.`
3. Back in the browser tab: select all (Cmd+A), copy (Cmd+C), paste into the Substack body (Cmd+V).
